The Challenge

Writing the Book Is Only Half the Journey

Millions of books are published every year. Quality alone does not guarantee that readers will find yours. A clear marketing plan closes that gap.

Where Many Authors Get Stuck

  • Not knowing who the ideal reader actually is
  • A book description that doesn't make readers want to click "buy"
  • Posting on social media with no direction or consistency
  • An Amazon listing with weak keywords and poor categories
  • Spending on ads without a clear plan or tracking
  • No email list, so every launch starts from zero
  • Feeling overwhelmed by too many marketing opinions

What Changes With a Proper Plan

  • You know exactly who your readers are and where they spend time
  • Your book description, bio and listing speak directly to them
  • Your content follows a written schedule you can follow with confidence
  • Your Amazon page is optimized to be found and to convert
  • Ad budgets are used deliberately and reviewed in writing
  • You build an email list that belongs to you
  • You have one clear document that tells you what to do next

Writing Guides

Book Description Formula

  1. Hook: one gripping line that stops the scroll.
  2. Story or promise: introduce the main character and conflict (fiction) or the problem you solve (non-fiction).
  3. Stakes: show what is at risk or what the reader will gain.
  4. Tone signal: let the voice match the book.
  5. Closing line: leave the reader curious and ready to buy.

Author Bio Formula

  1. Who you are: your name and what you write.
  2. Your book: title and one line about it.
  3. Credibility: relevant experience, awards or background.
  4. Personality: one human detail that makes you memorable.
  5. Where to find you: website or social handle.

Welcome Email Sequence

  1. Email 1: a warm welcome and the free item you promised.
  2. Email 2: your story and why you write.
  3. Email 3: a look at your book and a sample or excerpt.
  4. Email 4: behind-the-scenes or a reader-focused tip.
  5. Email 5: an invitation to read, review or reply.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we communicate?

All communication happens by email through Gmail. Plans, questions, approvals and reports are all written, so everything is saved in one place.

Do you hold meetings or calls?

No. My work is entirely written. This gives you time to read, think and reply at your own pace, and it keeps a permanent record of every decision.

What exactly will I receive?

Depending on the service, you will receive written plans, copy, calendars, templates, media materials and reports, all delivered as documents by email.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. Your written proposal will state the expected delivery time for each part of the work before you approve anything.

Will you help me find my audience?

Yes. Audience research is the starting point of every service. I study your genre, comparable books and reader behavior to define who your book is for.

Do you guarantee sales or bestseller status?

No. No ethical marketer can promise specific sales results. I guarantee honest work, clear plans and transparent reporting.

Which genres do you work with?

I work with both fiction and non-fiction across many genres. Each plan is adapted to the expectations of that genre's readers.

Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors?

Yes. I support independent, hybrid and traditionally published authors who want additional marketing support.

How soon will I see results?

Marketing builds over time. Some actions, like listing improvements, can have an early effect, while email lists, reviews and reputation grow steadily. Your reports will show progress honestly.

Do I need a big budget?

No. Services can be chosen individually, and the proposal will be matched to your needs. You can begin with a single service and add others later.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your book details, genre, goals and any existing materials such as a description, bio or links. I will list exactly what I need in the written intake.

Will the marketing sound like me?

Yes. I learn your voice from your writing and your answers, and everything is sent for your review before it is used.

How is pricing decided?

Each project receives a custom written quote based on the services you need and the scope of work. You will always see the full details before agreeing.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. The way revisions are handled will be explained clearly in your written proposal so expectations are shared from the start.

What is your refund policy?

Terms are written into each proposal so you know exactly what applies before you approve the work.

Who pays for advertising?

Ad spend is paid directly by you to the advertising platform, such as Amazon or Meta. It is separate from my service fee, and you keep full control of your ad account budget.

Do you buy or write fake reviews?

Never. I only recommend honest, platform-approved ways to gather reviews. Fake reviews put your book and reputation at risk.
